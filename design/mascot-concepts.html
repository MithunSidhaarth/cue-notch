Capsule creature, three techie directions

Vector concepts drawn in code, animated the way they would move in the notch. The bottom-right corner of each card shows the mark at 64, 32 and 16 px, and a notch mock-up sits at the top.

A · Terminal glyphA creature made of phosphor pixels. Its mouth is the cursor: it blinks while idle, types while thinking and streams while speaking. Pure developer culture, and the cheapest to render (rects only).
B · Hardware companionA graphite ceramic pill with a black glass face and dot-matrix amber eyes, like a Nothing or Teenage Engineering gadget. The equator seam is its status light, and the 6-dot grille is a real mic indicator. Best match for the notch itself.
C · Translucent engineeringA smoked-glass shell that shows a circuit board, a speaker coil and an amber core that beats while it works. The most "nerd-lovable" of the three. Detail drops out at small sizes, so the notch would use a simpler variant.
